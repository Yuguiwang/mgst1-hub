Mgst1 tissue expression hub (mouse, mm10)

Isoform models for mouse Mgst1 (variants v1/v3/v6/v7, mapped from user SnapGene CDS files to mm10 RefSeq) plus per-tissue polyA+ RNA-seq coverage (+strand) from ENCODE, all wild-type C57BL/6.

Adipose depots (orange): iWAT = subcutaneous adipose; eWAT/gWAT = gonadal fat pad; BAT = brown adipose; WAT = depot unspecified by ENCODE. rWAT is not available in ENCODE.

Coverage tracks stream directly from the ENCODE public data portal. Each track is auto-scaled.
